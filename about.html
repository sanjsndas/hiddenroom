About Hidden Room Stories

We are an independent editorial journal exploring the quiet architecture of everyday life — interior design, materials, space planning, and the art of living well. Founded in 2019, our mission is to publish thoughtful, in-depth writing that helps you see your home with fresh eyes.

By Elena Marchetti · Editor-in-Chief

Elena Marchetti

Founder & Editor-in-Chief

Elena is an architectural writer and former interior designer with over fifteen years of experience in residential practice. She founded Hidden Room Stories to create a space for slow, considered design journalism — away from trends and toward timeless principles.

Her writing has appeared in Dwell, Architectural Digest, and Remodelista. She lives in Copenhagen with her family.

What We Believe

Every article we publish is guided by a simple conviction: good design is a form of care. It begins with listening — to the site, to the climate, to the rhythms of the people who will inhabit the space.

◈

Depth over speed

We take time to research, interview, and refine. No clickbait, no listicles for the sake of volume.

◇

Practical wisdom

Our guides are grounded in real-world constraints — budgets, dimensions, climate, and craft.

◈

Quiet aesthetics

We celebrate restraint, material honesty, and spaces that support rather than dominate.

Our Story

  • 2019 Hidden Room Stories begins as a personal blog documenting a renovation of a 1920s apartment in Milan.
  • 2021 The site expands to include contributions from architects, designers, and craftspeople across Europe and North America.
  • 2023 We launch our weekly newsletter, now read by over 30,000 design enthusiasts.
  • 2025 Our first print anthology, The Quiet Architecture of Home, is published by a small independent press.
  • 2026 We continue to publish in-depth articles, guides, and interviews — always independent, always reader-supported.

Editorial Independence

Hidden Room Stories is entirely independent. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not publish advertorials disguised as editorial. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and a genuine belief in the value of good design.

When we use affiliate links — which is rare — we disclose it clearly at the top of the article. The small commission we earn helps fund our writing, but it never influences our editorial judgement. We only link to products or materials we would specify ourselves.
